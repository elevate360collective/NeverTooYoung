Common questions

What Parents Ask

Does my child need writing experience?

Not at all. The Founding Cohort welcomes young creators at every experience level. What matters is curiosity, imagination, and a story worth telling. Our coaches provide the structure and support to do the rest.

How much time is required each week?

Approximately 2–2.5 hours per week, including live coaching sessions and guided writing time. The program is designed to work within a family's existing schedule without feeling overwhelming.

What if my child has never written before?

That's completely fine. Many of our most compelling contributors are beginners. Our coaches meet students exactly where they are and guide them forward — at their own pace and in their own voice.

How are students selected?

Each application is reviewed individually. With enrollment limited to 12 students, we look for curiosity, a meaningful story, and a genuine desire to create — not prior writing experience or academic achievement.

Is parent involvement required?

Yes — and encouraged. For students ages 7–11, parent or guardian involvement is essential throughout the application and enrollment process. You'll receive regular updates and clear communication at every stage.

How does publishing work?

All publishing is managed through our partner Veritas Publishing House — including editing, design, ISBN registration, and worldwide distribution. Families are not responsible for any part of the publishing process.

Will my child receive individual coaching?

Yes. With only 12 students in the Founding Cohort, every participant receives personalized attention throughout. This is an intimate, high-touch creative experience — not a large group class.

What happens after the Founding Cohort?

Founding Cohort students become permanent members of the INK & IMPACT™ inaugural class — a distinction that belongs to them always. As the program grows, additional alumni opportunities will be available.
