The Never Too Young™ Collection · Volume One
A Professionally Published Anthology Featuring the Founding Cohort

Every Founding Cohort participant contributes to the inaugural Never Too Young™ Collection — a professionally published anthology celebrating twelve remarkable young lives.

Each young author is recognized with their own contribution, author credit and author biography — a permanent record of what made their story worth telling at this moment in their life.

Professional editing, design, ISBN registration and publishing support are provided through Veritas Publishing House.

The Bigger Picture

More Than a Writing Program.

Never Too Young™ was created around a bigger idea: when a young person learns to recognize the value of their own experiences, organize their thoughts, develop their voice and complete something meaningful, they're building skills that reach far beyond a book.

01
Voice

Learning to communicate ideas and experiences with clarity, confidence and authenticity.

02
Character

Building discipline, follow-through, responsibility and confidence in the process of creating something meaningful.

03
Creation

Taking an idea from its beginning all the way to a finished, published work — with guidance at every step.

The book is the outcome.
Who they become while creating it is the purpose.

The Four-Week Experience

The Journey from Idea to Author.

Develop their voice and turn those ideas into a guided written contribution.

04
